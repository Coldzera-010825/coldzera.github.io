# City climate samples, July 2022

Daily maximum and minimum 2 m air temperature from [Copernicus ERA5-Land via Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api), under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Each July 2022 curve uses the API's land sample for the city-centre coordinates and UTC daily intervals. These are point samples from a reanalysis. They do not characterize every neighborhood or establish a local heatwave.

The station point is a nearby location with verified temperature records for 13–19 July 2022 in [NOAA NCEI Global Hourly (ISD)](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/land-based-station/integrated-surface-database). It can lie outside the displayed administrative boundary. The chart keeps one quality-screened report per UTC hour, preferring reports with more valid fields and then those closest to the start of the hour. The location and distance are included in the city index. The hourly fields retain their original physical units after decoding. Missing hours and values are preserved.

The browser loads one city JSON when its observatory is opened and one station JSON on selection. The original source data is not copied into the site.
