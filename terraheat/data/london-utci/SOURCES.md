# Greater London pedestrian heat

Historical exploratory estimate for 18 July 2022 at 10:52 UTC. The model uses 5 m inputs from the Environment Agency's [2022 LiDAR DSM](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/cf3f1137-c12b-44a1-a835-e80fe4a60b92/lidar-composite-dsm-2022-1m) and [DTM](https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/01b3ee39-da3f-47b6-83da-dc98e73a461f/lidar-composite-dtm-2022-1m) (Open Government Licence), © Environment Agency copyright and/or database right 2022. Building outlines and candidate walking routes are from [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) via the [Geofabrik Greater London extract](https://download.geofabrik.de/europe/united-kingdom/england/greater-london.html), ODbL 1.0, snapshot replication time 2026-11-05 21:23 UTC. The derived network grid data retain ODbL attribution.

Weather forcing comes from 35 target locations in the [Open-Meteo historical archive](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api), CC BY 4.0. The 5 m radiation calculations use the experimental [SOLWEIG implementation](https://github.com/UMEP-dev/solweig) (0.1.0b96), following [Lindberg et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00484-008-0162-7) and the [UTCI operational procedure](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00484-011-0454-1). Canopy is inferred from the surface-minus-terrain height outside buildings. The 100 m results average samples along candidate walking routes and require at least 20 m of valid route per cell. Air temperature and wind are coarse model forcing within each model tile. Results have not been validated against independent pedestrian-height measurements.

The popup's optional land surface temperature is a separate 100 m area average from [USGS Landsat 9](https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/api/stac/v1/collections/landsat-c2-l2/items/LC09_L2SP_201024_20220718_02_T1), filtered with the product's pixel quality mask. The coarse 500 m overview is a route-length-weighted aggregation of valid 100 m cells. The continuous heatmap colour represents relative Mapbox rendering intensity.
